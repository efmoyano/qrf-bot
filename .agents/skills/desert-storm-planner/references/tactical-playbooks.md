# Desert Storm Tactical Playbooks & Callout Scripts

This reference provides detailed battle timeline scripts, opening orders, and tactical commands for alliance leadership during Desert Storm.

---

## 1. Match Timeline & Critical Milestones

```
[0:00 - Match Start] ------------------------------------------------->
   │ 
   ├── ⚡ MINUTE 0:00: STAGE 1 OPENS
   │   ├── Air Squads sprint to Info Center & Science Hub.
   │   ├── Primary Tanks march to Oil Refinery 1 (West) & 2 (East).
   │   └── Secondary Squads capture Field Hospitals 1-4.
   │
   ├── ⏳ MID-STAGE 1 (Before Stage 2 opens):
   │   ├── Confirm 100% control of Info Center (+10% point boost active).
   │   ├── Prepare relocations near Center for Stage 2.
   │   └── Science Hub active (1-minute teleport CD unlocked).
   │
   ├── ☢️ MINUTE X:00: STAGE 2 UNLOCKS
   │   ├── Nuclear Silo opens (+80 pts/s) -> Tier 1 Titans deploy immediately.
   │   ├── Arsenal opens (+15% Hero Buff) -> Missile squad assault.
   │   ├── Mercenary Factory opens (-15% Enemy Debuff) -> Flank capture.
   │   └── Random Oil Wells begin spawning across the map.
   │
   └── 🏁 ENDGAME:
       ├── Guard captured structures against last-minute overcharge theft.
       ├── Sweep scattered Oil Wells with rovers/reserves (+5 pts/s individual + alliance).
       └── Secure final point tally victory.
```

## 2. In-Game Alliance Callout Templates

Use these clear, formatted Discord or Alliance Chat announcements at each match transition:

### Announcement 1: 5 Minutes to Match Start (Safe Zone)
```text
📢 [DESERT STORM CALLOUT - PREPARATION]
All registered Main Squad players, enter the Safe Zone now!
🛡️ Tank Anchors: Pre-target Oil Refinery 1 (West) & Oil Refinery 2 (East).
✈️ Air Squads: Prepare instant sprint to Info Center (+10% point buff) and Science Hub (1m teleports).
Do not leave safe zones until 0:00!
```

### Announcement 2: Minute 0:00 (Stage 1 Opening)
```text
⚔️ [STAGE 1 IS LIVE - SECURE THE FLANKS!]
1. Air Squads: CAPTURE INFO CENTER NOW for the +10% point multiplier!
2. Tank Squads: Lock down Oil Refinery 1 and 2.
3. Peripheral squads: Occupy Field Hospitals 1, 2, 3, 4 for passive healing.
Report any contested structures immediately in voice/chat!
```

### Announcement 3: 1 Minute Before Stage 2 (Silo & Arsenal Prep)
```text
⚠️ [STAGE 2 INCOMING - REPOSITION TITANS!]
Nuclear Silo, Arsenal, and Mercenary Factory unlocking in 60 seconds!
☢️ Titan Squad (Alpha/Bravo): Teleport to Center perimeter around Nuclear Silo!
🚀 Missile Squad: Move on Arsenal for +15% alliance hero stat buff!
Refinery defenders: HOLD YOUR GROUND! Do not abandon the Refineries!
```

### Announcement 4: Stage 2 Active (Silo Control)
```text
☢️ [STAGE 2 ACTIVE - SECURE NUCLEAR SILO]
All designated Silo defenders: GARRISON NOW (+80 pts/s)!
Arsenal & Merc Factory teams: Lock down structures to activate the 30% combat stat swing!
Reserves/Standby: Harvest spawning Oil Wells across the map!
```

## 3. Structure Flip Defense & Overcharge Protection

* **Overcharge Warning**: Buildings held for long durations store accumulated overflow points.
* If an enemy attempts to flip an overcharged building:
  1. Identify incoming march timer.
  2. Call for fast Air squad counter-reinforcement or garrison stacking.
  3. If building cannot be held, immediately coordinate a counter-attack wave to re-capture it within 30 seconds to reclaim all accumulated points!
